
Usage Display Group (Pins 6 & 7)

Pin 6: CPU Usage LED Strip
Pin 7: GPU Usage LED Strip



Temperature Display Group (Pins 9, 10 & 11)

Pin 9: CPU Temp VU Meter
Pin 10: GPU Temp VU Meter
Pin 11: Temp RGB LED Strip (CPU and GPU combined in one strip)



Controls Group (Pins 2, 3, 4 & 12)

Pin 2: Data IR Receiver
Pin 3: Button 1 (Profile switch)
Pin 4: Button 2 (Brightness)
Pin 12: Button 3 (Test mode)
